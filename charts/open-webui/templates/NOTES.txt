Open WebUI deployed to namespace: {{ .Values.namespace }}

Open WebUI can take 3-5 minutes to become ready on first install (large image).
Use --wait to block until the pod is ready:

  helm install open-webui ./charts/open-webui --wait --timeout 5m

Or wait manually:

  kubectl wait -n {{ .Values.namespace }} \
    --for=condition=ready pod \
    --selector=app=open-webui \
    --timeout=300s
